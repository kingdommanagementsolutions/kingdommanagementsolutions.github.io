About Us

Kingdom Management Solutions, LLC is a software development and consulting company serving clients in every industry. Whether we are building a website for a local business, an app for a growing nonprofit or a game for families, our work is guided by one conviction: we are ministry-focused in all we do. That means integrity, careful craftsmanship and caring about your success as much as you do.
